## The curtain, honestly

It has never hung correctly until today, and the fall has never been seen
correctly at all.

1. **The pin was wrong.** Chaos sorts sim vertices column-by-column by low u,
   bottom-to-top within each column — not by mesh order. The "pinned heading"
   was holding the left vertical edge. Fixed, with an automated pin check that
   refuses to ship on failure.
2. **Every guided-fall number was benched on the mis-pinned cloth** — throw
   distance, drop speed, loft, gust. They point the right way; they are not
   trustworthy.
3. **The fall is genuinely broken.** Gary's "it seems to fall through the ground"
   is accurate.

   We briefly believed the recording method was at fault — that advancing the
   Sequencer playhead per tick teleport-reset the cloth. **A test disproved it.**
   With `teleport=False` and `teleport=True` the result is *indistinguishable*:
   both hang properly, then descend **taut and rigid** with the drape unchanged,
   and the cloth passes below the floor. `UChaosClothComponent` ignores
   `ETeleportType` altogether — it teleports only on its own `bTeleport`/`bReset`,
   `ForceNextUpdateTeleport`, or a move beyond `TeleportDistanceThreshold`
   (300 cm; the drop moves ≤62 cm per tick).

   **Unexplained:** why the descent is rigid. Suspects are `LinearVelocityScale`
   0.75, and the heading descending slowly enough in sim time that the cloth
   stays taut all the way down.

4. **Nothing has caught the correctly pinned cloth yet.** A separate floor-catch
   actor did *not*. Whether the in-actor `FloorCatch` does is unknown — it has
   only ever been seen working on the *mis-pinned* cloth.
5. **Use Movie Render Queue for anything simulated** — fixed timesteps, warm-up
   frames made for cloth. Still right, for its own reasons, not as a fix for this.
6. Whether scrubbing resets the cloth is **unmeasured**. It was inferred from the
   teleport theory and that theory is dead.

## Standing rules earned the hard way

- **A guard must live inside the thing it protects.** A check that ran once,
  elsewhere, earlier, is a hope. Two failures today had this exact shape.
- **No level save while any `ZZ_`/`TEMP` actor exists**, checked in the same
  script as the save.
- **Announce every bench** before and after: what, where, how many actors, how
  long. Off-stage still means *in the live level*.
- **Validate a metric against a case you can see** before trusting it. Two
  numbers misled us today while the images were unambiguous.
