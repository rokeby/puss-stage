## The finding that matters most

**There is no visible light shaft anywhere in Passage 1, and volumetric fog is
currently net-negative.** I had been briefing three streams all evening on "deep
night with volumetric beams" from persisted values and a saved read-back. The
render does not show it. That was my error: I asserted a look I had never seen.

A four-state A/B at beam bay 005 (means): baseline 4.367, **fog off 6.419**, light
function atlas on 4.563, that without MegaLights 4.422. Fog off is the brightest
state. The brightest half-percent of pixels is 54–59 in every state, so no faint
shaft is being missed.

Cause is **direction**, not the atlas and not density: with g=0.55 forward
scattering and every beam lamp throwing away from camera, only back-scatter is
seen — about 16× dimmer — while extinction applies to the whole corridor. Rig has
revised the P03 rig with 10 back-thrown lamps on even bays. Re-judge fog density
0.022 only after that lands.

## Bugs found that were already in the show

- **Every camera handover was a hard cut.** `SetViewTargetWithBlend` appeared
  nowhere in the plugin. The blend mechanism now exists and **ships at 0.0** —
  byte-identical to today — because nineteen seams should not change to a number
  nobody chose while nobody can see them. Your dial: Show Root → Transport →
  **Transport | Handover** → **Passage Entry Blend → Blend Seconds**, try **0.75**,
  and **Scene Entry Blend** at **0.4**.
- **Three camera authorities** were fighting over 19 segments. And during a blend
  `GetViewTarget()` reports the *outgoing* camera, so a blend added without
  finding that would have visibly done nothing.
- **Handoffs fired late** on any Scene sequence not starting at frame 0, and
  inconsistently, because two code paths used different arithmetic.
- **`FTransform::Equals(t, 0.5f)` applies the tolerance to quaternion components**,
  so a handoff camera re-aimed ~60° still counted as matching. A pure re-aim never
  registered as stale — a complete explanation for "the handover jumps and nobody
  knows why". **Not yet fixed**; it is in the handoff builder.
- **A lone mid-Passage camera key** redefines the segment end, which is where
  Scene handoffs snapshot. Adjusting framing silently changed a handoff two
  segments away. Look & camera's watch now catches it.
- A **latent unity-build collision** between two files untouched since the initial
  commit, exposed when new files regrouped the blobs.
- **A ceiling mask bug**: a BC5 normal map read as opacity returned 1.0
  everywhere, drawing solid black wedges on 24 P01 ceiling panels.
