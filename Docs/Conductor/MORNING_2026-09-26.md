### One interaction to know about before you turn a blend up

The Go1 receiver re-centres the dog on **view-target change**, and during a blend
the engine still reports the *outgoing* camera. So with a blend above zero the dog
steers the outgoing camera for the whole blend, then re-centres again when the
blend completes — and if its stick is deflected at that moment the view **steps**,
by up to 30.3° in Gaze mode. At a cut that step is hidden by the cut; after a blend
it lands mid-motion and is visible.

Look & camera has a fix (preferring the pending view target while a blend is in
flight, so the dog steers the incoming shot from the first frame). It is a no-op
at blend 0, so today stays byte-identical. **Approved, pending a build.**

**Your call, separately:** should a re-centre subtract the dog's current stick
offset, so a deflected stick never steps the view *even at a cut*? That changes
today's behaviour. There is a real argument against: in Gaze mode the dog's look is
meant to be **absolute**, and you may want the step at a cut precisely because it is
honest about where the animal is actually looking. That is a question about what
the dog *is* in this piece, not an engineering one.

## The finding that matters most

**There is no visible light shaft anywhere in Passage 1, and volumetric fog is
currently net-negative.** I had been briefing three streams all evening on "deep
night with volumetric beams" from persisted values and a saved read-back. The
render does not show it. That was my error: I asserted a look I had never seen.

A four-state A/B at beam bay 005 (means): baseline 4.367, **fog off 6.419**, light
function atlas on 4.563, that without MegaLights 4.422. Fog off is the brightest
state. The brightest half-percent of pixels is 54–59 in every state, so no faint
shaft is being missed.

Cause is **direction**, not the atlas and not density: with g=0.55 forward
scattering and every beam lamp throwing away from camera, only back-scatter is
seen — about 16× dimmer — while extinction applies to the whole corridor. Rig has
revised the P03 rig with 10 back-thrown lamps on even bays. Re-judge fog density
0.022 only after that lands.
