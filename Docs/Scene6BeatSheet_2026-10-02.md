## Beats (conductor's breakdown of the above; order and wording Gary's, numbering mine)

1. **Arrival:** the usual rig raise, but NO strike. It gets DIMMER. Drip, breathing, distant bees. A deep blackness swallows
   any sense of a box: total void.
2. **Still running:** Puss/the dog keeps running forward, not calming. Nothing comes out of the dark.
3. **First light:** the bees get closer; a single hanging bulb, dimly lit. The first bees (or fireflies: a glint of glow)
   zip fast past the camera, then more.
4. **The swarm:** we're sonically absorbed by it. Clickety-clackety, and an old lady counting.
5. **Looking up:** glimmers of her huge, looming body and face, hunched over us. Fingers pick at the air like an abacus, with
   the clacking. She's counting the bees, keeps getting it wrong, swears, groans, starts again. The swarm is impossibly
   chaotic to count.
6. **Puss looks around:** close, out-of-focus blurs of bees; a dimly lit swarm further out. The swarm is a Niagara cloud
   orbiting a moving target that darts around her. Her MetaHuman face only now and then, mostly very dark. Fog haloes the
   candle's light.
7. **Release:** Puss narrates. The old lady disappears; the bees dim away; the bulb goes out.
8. **Puss sleeps:** the boy takes the lead and starts counting, as if the idiot boy solves the bees, and Puss's search.
   **The end of the show.**
9. **Coda:** out of total darkness the lights come slowly back up. We're not in a box or a void but back in the stage machine:
   empty, theatrically unlit, idling. The old lady (or another character) comes on and switches the main lights off.
