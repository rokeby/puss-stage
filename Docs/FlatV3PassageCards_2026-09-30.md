# Flat V3 — passage cards

From Gary's moods, 30 September 2026. One card per passage, used to score and
shortlist sources and to brief the flats. Gary's words are quoted; everything
else is a proposal for him to cut.

**Rules for every passage** (Gary, 30 Sep):

- **Alternate organic and structural** — plants, rock or cloud against pedestal,
  scaffold or column — so the wings' in-and-out undulation becomes a rhythm of
  opposites.
- **Draped or covered structures** are the sweet spot: a distinctive form that
  is also a mystery (P06's scaffolding and draped shapes).
- **Every passage needs a structural voice.** Rich imagery without structure
  reads as wallpaper (P02's Rousseau plants: "lurid and rich but not so
  structurey").
- **Transitions are stepped, not graded.** A slow colour shift is lost to motion
  and blur. Change the *ratio* of the two families along the run (ABAB → ABBABB
  → BBB), so each step is legible.

**Single-family passages (Gary, 30 Sep, an option, not yet assigned):** not
every passage needs contrast. A passage could be *only* curtains, *only*
henge-like stones, or *only* slowly burning pyres that grow bigger as you go.
Such a passage lives by a **progression in one parameter** (size, spacing,
opening, burn) stepped along the run, since there is no contrast of form to
carry it. It also needs few unique assets, so each can be a hero. Owners if
chosen: curtains → Soft goods; fire → Effects sources (with the wing
material's existing per-instance `BurnProgress`); stones → this pipeline.

## P02 — forest path to the shrine

- **Mood:** darker, continuing "the uncanny animals story at the pond, next to
  the train wreck"; a "magic realist anthropocene feel of these mundane
  judgement forest creatures."
- **Now:** ~20 Rousseau-ish plant flats meant to turn more industrial, oily and
  dark along the passage. The transition isn't felt, and the dark plants are flat.
- **Families:** keep the Rousseau plants, enlarged. Alternate them with (a) a
  **red thorny, spiky, bushy** mass — bramble, briar, thistle, the Sleeping Beauty
  hedge — and (b) **brown biomechanical piping** introduced early and increasing
  in ratio toward the shrine.
- **Sources to try:** botanical engravings of thistles and brambles; Piranesi's
  *Carceri* for pipe and structure; anatomical vascular engravings as the pipe
  vocabulary (Giger-like without being Giger, which is copyrighted).
- **Palette:** deep green, oxblood red, rust brown, oily sheen increasing.

## P03 — the Protestant convent to the shore cult

- **Stones revised (Gary, 1 Oct): more ROUNDED stones and ABSTRACT rocks**,
  not angular trilithon geometry: boulders, eroded and holed menhirs, smooth
  lumpen forms, in folk-horror pseudo-realism.
- **Agreed 30 Sep: P03 carries the stones and the pyres.** A henge that grows
  (small, sparse standing stones, then heavier and closer, then trilithons) with
  pyres among them that burn bigger along the run: burning as forgetting, toward
  the shore cult. The convent's rectilinear planes can open the passage before
  the stones take over (a stepped ratio). Fire is Effects sources'.

- **Mood:** "a cool, protestant style and something quite architectural", a
  "sinister stiff bright rural feel with a touch of the Wicker Man"; arrives at
  "the shoreside cult and their singing clam and their song of forgetting".
- **Now:** a modern Swiss convent with clean lines, yellow curtains and bright
  windows, which ended up all rectangular and flat. What worked: "the
  rectilinear undulations had a slightly interdimensional feel as they shifted
  about on each other."
- **Keep:** the shifting rectilinear planes.
- **Break them with:** holes where light peeps through; rotten timber edges;
  exposed structure; and rural forms — hedgerows, barns, woven wattle and wicker
  (the Wicker Man link: woven hurdles are structural, see-through and sinister).
- **Sources to try:** Saenredam's whitewashed Dutch church interiors (paintings
  and drawings); Emanuel de Witte; barns and hayricks; woven fences and basketry.
- **Also (Gary, 30 Sep):** the Wicker Man, lithic prehistoric structures like
  Stonehenge, and "weird alien obelisk things". Sources: Aylett Sammes' 1676
  engraving of the wicker man (*Britannia Antiqua Illustrata*, the image the
  idea descends from); William Stukeley's 1740 Stonehenge and Avebury plates;
  Inigo Jones's Stonehenge; dolmens and menhirs (Carnac); Athanasius Kircher's
  hieroglyph-covered obelisks (*Obeliscus Pamphilius*, 1650) and Bernini's and
  Piranesi's obelisks for the alien note. Trilithons are ready-made wings:
  structural, with holes, and scale-free.
- **Palette:** cool whites, straw yellow, pale sky; hard, bright light.
- **Concept hook:** peeling as forgetting — earlier passages' posters surface
  through the skin here.

## P04 — the ruined church-school (Klara's scene)

- **Arrives at:** "an old stony school in which a pigeon is reading out names for
  some dancing lunatic children. Very cool English light."
- **Wings:** "old church architecture, broken down, ruinous… Coventry cathedral
  but more baroque maybe. Heavy, leaden, medieval, Italian, roughly. Light
  streaming through windows and breakages."
- **Now:** "total crap"; length and style arbitrary. Redo completely.
- **Families:** broken piers, arches and tracery (structural) / collapse, rubble,
  ivy and fallen masonry (organic-ish decay). Window and breakage holes are
  essential — they are the light's way in.
- **Sources to try:** Monsù Desiderio (François de Nomé), collapsing fantastical
  churches; Hubert Robert's ruined galleries; Piranesi's ruins; Pannini; Gothic
  tracery engravings; pass-1/2 P04 "impossible palace" and grotto picks.
- **Palette:** leaden grey stone, cold daylight, black lead.
- **Not wings:** the passage's length should follow its music (ShowCreativeTodo
  D.4). That's for the conductor, not this pipeline.

## P05 — bucolic mountains and the village

- **Mood:** "whimsy, a bit sugary", the "Basile picaresque strange but lovely
  tone" of the original *Tale of Tales*; the bickering mountains eventually
  flatten the village.
- **Now:** the clouds are good. Keep them, enlarge them, and add to them.
- **Add:** a ground-level anchor. Bamboo poles and scaffolding holding the clouds
  up. Some darker clouds held up by big flat zigzag lightning — "not threatening
  but fun". Drifting feathers, butterflies, cute mice on the ground.
- **Avoid:** more greenery ("we've had a lot already"). Keep the clouds more abstract.
- **Ending (agreed 30 Sep):** as the mountains crush the village, the wings
  **topple flat**, hinged at the base, with the poles left standing. This needs
  the proposed topple mechanism.
- **Sources to try:** emblem-book and allegorical thunderbolts (stylised zigzag
  bolts); Maria Sibylla Merian's butterflies; Baroque animal painting for mice
  and birds; bamboo in public-domain East Asian prints; the existing cloud flats.
- **Palette:** sugared pastels, bright sky, cream and blue.

## P06

- **What works:** "various forms of scaffolding and draped over structures that
  are both mysterious and have distinctive form." Acceptable as it is.
- **Gary loves scaffolding "for their form"** (his night photos, 30 Sep:
  `DerivedAssets/FlatV3/GaryPhotos/Scaffolding_2026-09-30/`): ladder frames and
  X-braces in a lit bay grid, one lamp per bay, black netting veiling the
  building behind, and a stepped, swagged top edge against the sky.
  Scaffolding is thin members, so it should be **real instanced geometry with a
  netting layer**, not a painted cutout. Best combined with a painted wing
  half-sheathed in scaffold and net: the building under construction, veiled.
- **Mood / arrives at (Gary, 1 Oct):** the METAFICTIONAL passage. The robot
  dog / picaresque cat is about to enter the void. Not a jaunty passage but "a
  deterioration of what we got used to before". Arrives at S06: the void, the bees
  and the old woman.
- **The arc, stepped (each stage a ratio step, not a blend):**
  1. **Cute, exotic, grand and extravagant:** "orientalist perhaps". Chinoiserie
     is itself a European Baroque stage fantasy of the East, which suits a
     passage about the illusion breaking. Sources to try: Pillement's
     chinoiserie, Boucher and Huet's singeries and chinoiseries, pagoda and
     pavilion designs, garden follies. Check the existing
     `GeneratedWingsV2_Chinoiserie` set on disk first.
     **Bronze tree scale (Gary, 1 Oct):** the fine branches may read too small on a
     10 m wing, so go for a LARGER tree: a massive trunk, few thick branches, big
     birds and masks.
     **Add a hint of the alien (Gary, 1 Oct): Sanxingdui.** The bronze masks
     with protruding stalk eyes and the bronze sacred trees. The bronze tree is
     a ready-made wing form, structural and organic at once. Rights: the objects
     are ancient, but PHOTOGRAPHS of them are not public domain (Wikimedia
     copies are mostly CC BY-SA, whose share-alike is risky for the show). Use
     photos as REFERENCE only and MODEL the forms in Blender (masks and tree as
     painted/bronze flats), or use photos Gary takes himself.
  2. **Scaffolding appears** among the grand flats (the scaffold bay, netting).
  3. **Crumble and peel:** the billboard peel amount STEPS UP along the run, then
     flats lose pieces and frames show. This needs a per-instance peel value (a
     new custom-data slot; Wing materiality's call).
  4. **Fire and strange drapes:** BurnProgress on chosen flats (Effects sources)
     and drapes (Soft goods).
  5. **Monochrome or infrared-type vision** by the end of the path: a camera post
     process, Look & camera's.
  6. **The curtain smokes and burns; Puss walks through into total darkness.**
     The big hangar is heard coming in, but nothing is seen, then almost nothing,
     then bees.
- **For Gary with Cameron:** the timing of the walk and its pauses, and the
  background sound across the darkness. The stage lengths should follow that.
- **Owners beyond this pipeline:** fire (Effects sources), drapes and the burning
  curtain (Soft goods), the vision change (Look & camera), sound (Cameron).
- **Work:** a V3 rebuild on the approved art.
