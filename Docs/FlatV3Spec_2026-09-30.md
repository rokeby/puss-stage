# Flat V3 — spec and phased plan for stage wings and other flats

Agreed with Gary, 30 September 2026. Supersedes the V2 profile in
`Docs/WingGenerationConventions.md` for **new** flats; V2 and legacy meshes stay
as they are until their passage is rebuilt. Evidence tags: *measured* = read
back or computed from files, *code* = read in source, *inferred* = reasoning,
not checked.

## Why a new spec

- **Blur in motion.** All 186 wing textures in P01/P02 have `TMGS_NO_MIPMAPS`
  (*measured*, `Saved/CodexRenderDiag/p01_p02_render_audit.json`, 21 Sep). None of
  the 148 wing textures is power-of-two; 86 use `TC_EditorIcon` and all 86 have
  a side that is not a multiple of 4 (*measured*,
  `Saved/CodexWingMateriality/texture_sizes.json`). Without mips a texture
  aliases; TSR converges when still and smudges in motion (*inferred*, matches
  Gary's "sharp paused, smudged moving, floor sharp", RUNTHROUGH 29 Sep).
  Secondary suspects: engine-default motion blur (*config*); per-frame
  `MarkRenderStateDirty` on moving wing ISMs
  (`InfinityStagePassageActor.cpp:1461`, *code*; effect on velocity *inferred*).
- **In-level adjustment.** Every mesh is built 10 m tall (P03 scaled 0.8, P05
  0.4, P05 tops 0.2 in `LocalTransform`); the baked yaw leaves Front facing +X,
  so on 18 Sep nearly every L/R panel was set to `Rotate180AroundUp`; the pivot
  is the silhouette's bottom centre, which moves with transparent margins
  (*code* `InfinityStagePassageActor.cpp:1015-1020`, `:86-124`; *measured*
  18 Sep rows dump).
- **Backs** show the Front image mirror-reversed (same MI, same planar UVs;
  `blender_png_to_wing.py:435-446`, *code/inferred*).
- **Resolution.** Art is ~1024×1536 on 10 m panels: ~6.5 mm/texel against
  0.6–1.2 mm/px on screen at 1–2 m (`Docs/WingMateriality.md:268`).

## Gary's decisions (30 Sep)

- **Finish:** billboard skin is the default; the oily-paint master
  (`M_Wing_PaintedSurface_Master`) is kept for passages that want it.
- **Backs:** raw aged timber. Stencils and chalk scrawls are welcome but **faint
  and sparse** — never prominent.
- **Art source, in order of preference:** (1) public-domain / CC0 high-resolution
  museum scans of Baroque paintings and Baroque stage design; (2) image
  generation for cleanup, extension past the cut line and handedness variants;
  (3) whole-image generation only where nothing suitable exists. Paid API use
  only after a firm per-batch cost is shown; if too expensive, a free upscaler
  (install needs Gary's OK).
- **Gallica/BnF scans are reference only (Gary, 1 Oct):** their free reuse is
  non-commercial. The Torelli *Andromède* (Acts 1 and 2) and *Finta pazza* sheets
  are marked `use_restriction` in `D:\PussStageSources\FlatV3\MANIFEST.json`.
  They are studied, not staged; the Met's CC0 Torelli/Silvestre engravings are
  the stageable alternative.
- **Credit cap (Gary, 30 Sep):** no image generation or paid API spend beyond the
  first pilot wing pair. Anything past that pair waits for Gary's explicit OK,
  with the cost stated first.
- **Existing passages:** P01 and P06 are acceptable. P02 (plants), P03 (Swiss
  modern white walls) and P05 (clouds) need more creative thought. P04 is
  "utterly unconsidered" and is redone completely.
- **Poles:** yes; wide flats get two. Their occasional visibility is what makes
  the floor slots make sense.
- **Order:** sourcing first; then one P01 pilot pair; then the least-designed
  passages (P04 first, then P02/P03/P05 as their ideas are settled); P01/P06
  rebuilds after that.

## Design principles

See the [passage cards](FlatV3PassageCards_2026-09-30.md) for Gary's quoted
moods and passage-specific rules. Alternate organic and structural families;
favour draped structures; give every passage a structural voice; and make
transitions by stepped changes in family ratio, never by a graded blend.

A wing should read as one mass with a straight backbone and a lively inward
edge, with features reaching toward the camera's path and see-through holes for
light. Keep the spine darker and the reveal lighter. Prefer scale-free subjects,
develop families rather than 48 unique images, and reserve horizontal masses
for borders.

## The physical flat

- **Profile board** ~1.8 cm, cut to the silhouette. Billboard skin on the front.
- **Timber frame** generated from the silhouette: a stile on the backbone
  (offstage) edge and at each pole; rails every ~150 cm clipped to the interior;
  diagonal braces on panels wider than ~3 m; ply corner blocks; members ~5×10 cm;
  set back ≥ 8 cm from the cut edge. Total depth ≈ 12 cm.
- **Back of board:** raw aged ply, front-paint overspray at the edges, faint
  stencil (e.g. `PUSS · P01 · L07`) and sparse chalk marks.
- **Edge:** ply laminations plus a paper lip, frayed where the skin peels.
- **Poles:** one up to ~4 m width, two above; clamped to stiles; from ~100 cm
  below the base (into the groove) to just above the top. **Superseded on how:**
  poles are a separate shared mesh placed at the card's `Pole_n` sockets and
  moved with the card (see "Built for movement"), not baked into the card.
  Borders (`_C`) carry a bottom-edge pipe and two lift-line stubs instead.
  **Rig's conditions (30 Sep):** (a) every pole sits on the card's pivot line,
  where the Structure builds the groove — i.e. the V3 origin plane; (b) the pole
  foot reaches **below the floor top (boards at z 0)** into the groove, however
  high the card's own base sits, so it stands in the groove, not on the boards;
  (c) a mixed stage (V3 flats with poles beside legacy cards without) is a look
  state for Gary to judge with the pilot pair.
- **Silhouette is geometry, not alpha.** Smoothed to a jigsaw-cuttable line (no
  feature under ~3 cm at final size); art bleeds past the line. The Front
  material is **opaque**, not masked.

## Placement contract — zero adjustment in the level

- Built at **final size, scale 1**; the manifest states height in cm per
  passage/size class. `LocalTransform` scale is always (1,1,1).
- **Origin** = pole line (X) × backbone edge (Y) × floor level (Z). Groove,
  pole and origin coincide at the station; the painted face sits ~15 cm toward
  the approaching camera. Borders: origin at bottom-edge centre.
- **Front faces the approaching camera** (−X in the Passage frame) with
  `FacingPolicy` identity and `bFlipImageHorizontally` false. L extends +Y from
  its backbone, R extends −Y; image U runs +Y on both, so handedness is right by
  construction. (Direction *inferred* from the 18 Sep facing pass — the pilot
  must measure it.)
- The pipeline writes a Wing Library entry per mesh (FrontAxis, identity
  PlacementAdjustment) so nothing is hand-set.
- **Acceptance:** one pair at `(0, ±lateral, 0)`, rotation 0, scale 1 renders
  correctly, read back from Unreal, before any batch.

## Billboard skin

- **Sheets:** ~100×150 cm with 2–3 cm overlaps, each a few mm out of register.
- **Unevenness:** paste wrinkles and a few bubbles from the paper — never height
  derived from the picture's luminance.
- **Peel:** at seams, edges and the base; beneath, an older poster, white backing
  with paste stains, or grey primer; curls have real height.
- **Sheen:** satin (roughness ~0.4–0.55); paste-soaked glossier, faded matte; no
  mirror clear-coat.
- **Print screen:** coarse ~4–5 mm halftone in panel centimetres, faded by pixel
  footprint so it never shimmers.
- Resolution-independent detail lives in the material; per-asset maps are the
  sheet layout, peel/damage masks and paper height/normal.

## Texture rules

- Power-of-two only. Full mips. Never `TC_EditorIcon`.
- **Per unique flat (revised 1 Oct, performance review), about 16 MB instead of ~43 MB:**
  - Colour (sRGB, BC7): 2048×4096 standard; 4096×8192 for hero flats only, at
    the Opening and interactions.
  - **One packed skin mask, 1024×2048 (linear, BC7):** R = continuous peel
    AMOUNT (0 intact to 1 fully peeled; never layer IDs, which filter into a
    false white-backing fringe at every peel edge, per Wing materiality),
    G = paper height, B = roughness, A = paste-soak gloss/AO. The under-layer
    (white backing / older poster / primer) is chosen PER SHEET in the shader,
    from a hash of the sheet cell in UV1 metres, with the sheet-grid parameters
    stored on the MI. Paper features are centimetre-scale, so half resolution
    is enough.
  - Normal (BC5): 1024×2048, derived from the paper height.
  - Halftone and paper fibre are PROCEDURAL in the shader (panel cm, faded by
    pixel footprint): no textures. Timber/ply/steel tiling sets are shared.
- **Families over uniques:** reuse a flat at several positions in a passage
  rather than making every one unique; it divides texture memory.
- **No POM by default.** Depth comes from the real frame, board edge and flutter
  flaps. POM only by exception, with a reason recorded.
- UV0 photographic. **UV1 in metres on ALL slots** (confirmed with Wing
  materiality, 1 Oct). On Front and Flap, UV1 = panel-plane metres (U from mesh
  Y, V from mesh Z), used for the sheet-grid hash, halftone and fibre. A flap
  carries the UV0/UV1 of the board point it peeled from, so its sheet and
  print match the board beneath. On Timber and Edge, UV1 is box-mapped metres.
- **Four material slots (revised 1 Oct, with Wing materiality): Front, Flap,
  Timber, Edge.**
  - **Front:** opaque, no WPO, ever. Any WPO on a primitive invalidates its VSM
    shadow pages every frame, so board fronts stay WPO-free.
  - **Flap:** the same skin with a WPO static switch; flutter flaps only.
  - **Timber:** covers the board back, frame and hardware, so a turning wing
    still shows a real back.
  - **Edge:** the board edge.
- **Vertex colour channels per slot, so they never collide:**
  - Flap VC.R = flutter weight (0 fixed at the hinge edge, 1 free);
  - Timber VC.R = material ID (0 frame softwood, 0.5 ply back, 1 steel
    hardware), Timber VC.G = per-member random for variation;
  - Front and Edge carry no VC meaning.
- **Burn:** the master is opaque; only MIs Gary chooses to burn get a BlendMode
  override to Masked.
- **Axes and dimensions (for the Edge and Timber materials):** in the exported
  mesh, board thickness runs along X (the Front faces -X); the board is 1.8 cm;
  frame members are about 5 x 10 cm, with the 10 cm along X behind the board,
  set back >= 8 cm from the cut edge; total depth is about 12 cm.
- **LOD1 beyond ~15 m:** drop the frame and hardware (backs are not visible there).
- **Scaffold-type structures:** netting is a dark gauze up close that turns
  solid at distance, not an alpha weave (alpha weaves shimmer under TSR);
  this is tested in the pilot. Lamps are emissive bulbs only; real lights only
  where Rig hangs them.

## Built for movement (Gary, 30 Sep)

- **Flutter:** peel flaps, loose paper corners, drapes and pennants are separate
  small mesh parts in the Flap slot, carrying the vertex-colour weight (Flap
  VC.R: 0 = fixed, 1 = free). The material sways them with world-position offset
  driven by that weight; the Front slot never has WPO. No cloth simulation. The flap's hinge edge has weight 0 so it never
  tears away from the board.
- **Poles are a separate shared mesh, not baked** (revised 30 Sep, from Rig's
  contract): a mechanism moves one ISM instance per card, so a baked pole would
  topple with its card. The card carries sockets `Pole_1` (and `Pole_2` when it
  is wide) at the pole positions; the passage actor instances a shared pole mesh
  there and moves it with the card's slide, but not its topple. All the earlier
  pole rules (pivot line, foot below z 0, one or two by width) still hold.
  Rig's call: one pole mesh, **scaled in Z** per card, in its own ISM per
  passage, taking the card's slide translation in the same batch (so velocity
  is correct under TSR). Its material therefore tiles V by object scale, so
  wear and banding never stretch.
- **Sockets (agreed with Rig, 30 Sep):** `Hinge` sits at the card's lowest point
  on the pole-line plane, with socket +X along the base edge as the topple
  rotation axis. `Pivot` sits on the vertical centre axis for turning wings,
  with socket +X up. Cards without them fall back to bounds.
- **Trap cards:** the Structure's slots are 8 cm wide (Rig, measured), so a
  trap card is a slim variant: the 1.8 cm board on slim battens, ≤ 6 cm total,
  with no full frame. Otherwise the slot widens at trap stations (conductor's call).
- **Burn-ready:** front UVs stay compatible with the wing material's
  per-instance `BurnProgress` contract (`Docs/WingMateriality.md`, "Per-instance
  custom data").
- **Periaktos (turning wing) type:** a three-faced flat on a central pivot; one
  face may be the timber back. Asset type defined here; the motion needs the
  mechanism below.
- **Mechanisms not yet in the code:** today a wing only slides inward and out
  (a cycle rate and phase), and a border flies on Z (`InfinityStageTypes.h:614-633`).
  Topple, turn and rise-through-a-trap are proposed to the conductor as new
  mechanism kinds, owned outside this pipeline.

## Other flats — same pipeline, different mount

- **Wing (L/R):** backbone stile + poles.
- **Border (C):** bottom pipe + lift-line stubs.
- **Sea rows:** painted cutout wave rows from an image (Baroque cutout sea, not a
  parametric curve). Art repeats seamlessly along the row; 2–3 rows at depth, each
  on a bottom rail with a crank mount; the staggered back-and-forth is a Show
  mechanism setting.
- Scene 4 architectural panels, shadow-puppet profiles, backdrops.
- **Scene 3 hangar (Rig's numbers, 1 Oct; geometry declaration-proven from
  `hangar_s3_plan.json` / `hangar_build.py`):**
  - **Backdrop:** the far wall's inner face is 20.0 m wide x 30.0 m tall, 65 m
    from the camera stop (about 81 m from ClamCam2). A 4096x8192 colour texture
    (2:1 portrait, POT) is ample at that distance. Tonal sea paintings are
    landscape-format, so the painting sits low (horizon in the lower third) and
    the fog-sky extends upward. The top 18 m is seen past the lattice. The
    backdrop is a child MI of `MI_Billboard_Front` with `SkinColor` swapped per
    hangar, so one surface serves every hangar.
  - **Wave rows:** keyed static-mesh actors on the S3 hangar root, NOT Passage
    occurrences (wing mechanisms only evaluate inside their Passage segment,
    `InfinityStagePassageActor.cpp:1166`, per Rig). Sequencer-keyed +/-Y slides,
    alternating and delayed per row. Each row is two halves of >= 12 m
    overlapping, for a +/-1.5 m slide. Heights step up toward the back. Rows
    stand 30-60 m from the stop across the full 20 m. Shallow-framed V3 boards
    with the billboard/painted-wood front; no Hinge/Pivot/Pole sockets.

## Texture library

Timber, ply, pole steel/brass, hangar metal (riveted painted sheet, rust bloom),
hemp. Start from CC0 scans (Poly Haven, ambientCG); normalise tile size in cm,
map packing and naming; age to the show's register; record licence per file.
Generation only for overlays (stencils, old-poster fragments, grime). Lands in
`DerivedAssets/TextureLibrary`; Unreal import needs an owner per family.

## Unreal integration (Wing materiality, 1 Oct)

`M_Wing_Billboard_Master` is built (compiles clean) in
`/Game/Scenes/Wings/PaintedSurface/Billboard`. Per-flat MIs are CHILDREN of:
- `MI_Billboard_Front` for the Front slot (opaque, no WPO);
- `MI_Billboard_Flap` for the Flap slot (Flutter on, two-sided).

Per-flat parameters:
- `SkinColor` (sRGB), `SkinMask` and `SkinNormal` (BC5), all on UV0;
- the sheet grid on UV1 metres: `SheetWidthM`, `SheetHeightM`,
  `SheetOverlapM`, `RowOffset`;
- `UVSpanUCm` / `UVSpanVCm` only if the flat will burn.

**Import `SkinMask` with compression `TC_Masks` (sRGB off).** The master's
sampler is MASKS, so any other compression fails the MI's compile. This
supersedes "BC7" for the mask above. Timber and Edge masters follow once the
texture library's `library.json` is ready.

## Codex working rules (1 Oct, performance review)

- **Mechanical work runs outside the AI loop.** Codex writes or fixes a tool,
  then batch work (downloads, cutting, flat builds, skin maps) runs as plain
  Python with logs and no agent supervising it. The first download run took
  228 min, mostly an agent awake through Wikimedia's 600 s holds (measured from
  run logs).
- **Downloads: home institutions first, Wikimedia Commons last** (or never for
  originals); per-host backoff; one request at a time.
- **Model loads once per batch:** SAM 2.1 and Grounding DINO stay resident across
  all images.
- **GPU jobs one at a time** (renders, segmentation, upscaling); network and CPU
  jobs may run alongside.
- **Reports:** full evidence-tiered reports for decisions; routine batch runs emit
  a data file plus a five-line summary.
- **Generation stays minimal:** museum scans for art, Blender for structures,
  Nano Banana (manual, Gary) only for colour on sketches, paid generation only for
  the pilot pair under the credit cap.
